6. Diagnostic Assessment Criteria & Thresholds (IEEE C57.106 / IEC 60422)
Normal / Satisfactory Condition
BDV ≥ 45 kV, Moisture ≤ 20 ppm, Acidity ≤ 0.10 mgKOH/g, IFT ≥ 30 mN/m. Oil is in excellent dielectric state. Continue regular routine sampling.
Fair / Questionable Condition
BDV 40–44 kV, Moisture 21–30 ppm, Acidity 0.11–0.20 mgKOH/g, IFT 25–29 mN/m. Moderate oil degradation. Plan re-filtration / dehydration.
Poor / Action Required
BDV < 40 kV, Moisture > 30 ppm, Acidity > 0.20 mgKOH/g, IFT < 25 mN/m. High risk of dielectric breakdown and sludge formation. Immediate oil reclamation/replacement.
