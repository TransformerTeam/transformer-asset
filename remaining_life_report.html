• Technical Methodology & Mathematical Formulation (CIGRE 761 / IEEE C57.152)
1. SINTEF Winding Ageing Model (Thermal-Chemical Kinetic Degradation)
Chain Scissions: η = DP₀ · [ A_Oxi · e^(-E_Oxi / RT) + A_Hyd · e^(-E_Hyd / RT) ] · t
Current DP: DP_t = DP₀ / (η + 1)
• Parameters: DP₀ = Dynamic Current DP, R = 8.314 J/(mol·K), E_Hyd = 128 kJ/mol, E_Oxi = 89 kJ/mol. A_Hyd = 4.10×10¹⁰ h⁻¹ (<0.5%), 1.50×10¹¹ h⁻¹ (0.5–2.0%), 4.50×10¹¹ h⁻¹ (≥2.0%). A_Oxi = 4.60×10⁵ h⁻¹ (O₂ ≥ 7000 ppm).
2. Indirect DP Estimation from Furan 2-FAL (Dominelli / CIGRE 761 Correlation)
Dominelli Correlation: log₁₀(2-FAL_ppm) = 1.51 - 0.0035 · DP
Estimated DP: DP = [ 1.51 - log₁₀(2-FAL_ppm) ] / 0.0035
• Note: 2-FAL concentration is converted from ppb to ppm (e.g. 9.3 ppb = 0.0093 ppm) before logarithmic calculation.
3. Weibull Cumulative Probability of Failure (PoF) Fleet Risk Model
Weibull PoF Formula: PoF(t) = 1 - e^[ -(t / α)^β ]
Aging Extrapolation: DP_future = 1 / [ 1/DP_curr + k_yearly · Δt ]
• Parameters: Characteristic life scale α = 40–45 years, shape parameter β = 2.0–4.0 (Wear-out aging phase bathtub curve).

• Engineering Recommendations & Strategic Asset Action Plan
• Short-term Actions (0–12 Months): Perform annual DGA and Furan 2-FAL screening. If moisture in oil indicates paper moisture > 1.5%, plan for online oil dehydration or vacuum hot oil purification to retard hydrolysis.
• Medium-term Actions (1–3 Years): Maintain transformer cooling system (radiator fans and oil pumps) to restrict winding hotspot temperature below 95 °C under peak loading.
• Long-term Asset Planning (3–5+ Years): Review Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) fleet replacement horizon prior to reaching the mechanical end-of-life threshold (DP < 200).
