Standards & Mathematical Formulation (IEEE C57.152 / IEC 60076-1)
1. Voltage Ratio Tolerance: Ratio error must not exceed ±0.50% of nameplate ratio on all tap connections (IEEE C57.152 Cl. 6.5 & IEC 60076-1 Cl. 9.3).
2. Phase-to-Phase Deviation: Maximum difference among 3 phases at any tap shall be ≤ 0.50%.
3. Phase Displacement Angle: Should match vector group nominal angle within ±0.5°.
