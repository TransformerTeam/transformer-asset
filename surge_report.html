6. Diagnostic Assessment Criteria & Guidelines (IEEE C57.152 / IEC 60099-4)
Good / Normal Condition
Leakage current ≤ 0.50 mA, Watt Loss within nameplate limits, Insulation Resistance ≥ 10,000 MΩ. Phase variance ≤ 10%.
Warning / Marginal Condition
Leakage current elevated (0.51 - 1.00 mA), IR between 1,000 - 9,999 MΩ, or phase-to-phase variance > 15%. Plan inspection.
Critical / Replace Arrester
Leakage current > 1.00 mA, IR < 1,000 MΩ, abnormal thermal signature, or physical tracking. Replace surge arrester immediately.
