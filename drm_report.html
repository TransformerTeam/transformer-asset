7. Engineering Assessment & Recommendations
Diagnostic Summary Dynamic Resistance Measurement (DRM / Dyn. OLTC-scan) of the High Voltage winding and On-Load Tap Changer was successfully performed across all 19 tap positions in both Up/Down directions at 5.0 A DC test current. The corrected winding resistance at 75°C ranges from 0.635 Ω (Tap 10) to 0.771 Ω (Tap 1 & 19) with symmetrical linear tap steps. The maximum phase-to-phase resistance deviation is 0.63% (well within the IEEE C57.152 limit of ≤ 1.00%). Dynamic current ripple during tap switching transitions demonstrates smooth, consistent behavior ranging between 50.9% and 69.8% with zero contact discontinuities or open circuits (Bounce = 0.00 ms). The diverter contacts, selector switches, and transition resistors are operating with high mechanical and electrical integrity.
OLTC Condition Rating NORMAL (HEALTHY) Score: 5 / 5 (IEEE C57.152 & CIGRE A2.34)
Corrective Action & Plan OLTC switching mechanism and diverter contacts are in excellent condition. Maintain normal operating routine. Continue standard oil sampling/DGA on the OLTC diverter compartment per PM schedule. Next planned DRM assessment during the next major turnaround.
