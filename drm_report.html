7. Engineering Assessment & Recommendations
Diagnostic Summary Dynamic Resistance Measurement (DRM) of the On-Load Tap Changer (OLTC Type: V III 200D) was performed across all 17 tap positions in both Raise (1 → 17) and Lower (17 → 1) operational directions. The measured switching times range between 43.8 ms and 47.1 ms (well within the IEEE standard window of 30.0 - 70.0 ms). No circuit discontinuities, micro-bounces, or contact interruptions were detected (Discontinuity = 0.00 ms). The transition resistors measured 6.52 Ω – 6.61 Ω (deviation +0.61% vs. 6.54 Ω nameplate baseline, within ±10% limit). All three phases exhibit high synchronicity (Δt ≤ 1.8 ms), indicating proper energy accumulator spring operation and excellent contact integrity.
OLTC Condition Rating NORMAL (HEALTHY) Score: 5 / 5 (IEEE C57.152 & CIGRE A2.34)
Corrective Action & Plan OLTC switching mechanism and diverter contacts are in sound mechanical and electrical condition. Maintain standard Preventive Maintenance (PM) and perform DGA/Oil dielectric breakdown screening on the diverter tank per routine schedule. Next planned DRM assessment due in 2029 (or after 80,000 operations).
