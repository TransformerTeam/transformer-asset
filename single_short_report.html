Standards & Mathematical Formulation (IEEE C57.152 Cl. 6.6)
1. Measured Impedance: Single-phase equivalent impedance is calculated by applying single-phase AC voltage and measuring current with secondary short-circuited:
Z_k = V_k / I_k (Ω)
2. Phase-to-Phase Deviation: Maximum percentage deviation among 3 phases at identical tap connection:
% Dev = ((Z_max - Z_min) / Z_avg) × 100%
3. Factory / Baseline Deviation: Difference from FAT baseline:
% FAT Dev = (|Z_measured - Z_factory| / Z_factory) × 100%
